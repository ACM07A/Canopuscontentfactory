# Canopus Care — Detailed Vertical Page Blueprints

Each final page should become a 3,600–6,000 word medically reviewed resource, not a thin template.

## Hip replacement
Search: hip replacement India, cost, hospitals, surgeons, recovery.
Unique: hip arthritis/differentials, when arthroplasty is considered, non-operative care, total replacement, approach terminology without claiming one is universally superior, implants/bearings, DVT, dislocation precautions, rehab, travel. CTA: send hip X-rays and prior notes.

## Revision knee/hip
Unique: loosening, infection, instability, fracture, wear, prior implant details, infection work-up, bone loss, staged revision concepts, longer stay and why revision expertise matters. CTA: send prior operative note, implant stickers and current X-rays.

## Heart valve surgery
Unique: valve symptoms, echocardiogram, severity, repair vs replacement, mechanical vs bioprosthetic considerations, surgery vs transcatheter options, anticoagulation implications, recovery, Heart Team, travel. No patient-specific recommendation.

## TAVR
Unique: aortic stenosis, echo, CT planning, Heart Team, transcatheter procedure, device cost, vascular access, pacemaker possibility, follow-up, travel. Use only hospitals with current official TAVR programs.

## Breast cancer
Unique: symptoms, biopsy, pathology, ER/PR/HER2, stage, breast conservation vs mastectomy, sentinel node, reconstruction, systemic therapy, HER2-directed treatment, endocrine treatment, radiation, neoadjuvant sequence, fertility discussion where relevant, cost by component. CTA: pathology + biomarkers + scans.

## Lung cancer
Unique: CT, biopsy, histology, stage, molecular testing, surgery, radiation, systemic treatment, targeted treatment, immunotherapy, lung function, travel with respiratory symptoms.

## Prostate cancer
Unique: PSA, biopsy, grade/stage, surveillance concept, surgery, radiation, hormonal/systemic treatment, robotic surgery, continence/sexual-function discussion, long-term PSA follow-up.

## Bone marrow/stem-cell transplant
Unique: autologous vs allogeneic, donor matching, conditioning, infusion, engraftment, infection risk, GVHD, post-transplant monitoring, longer international stay, accommodation hygiene, current cost. High-intensity medical review.

## Kidney transplant
Separate current legal/compliance review mandatory. Cover end-stage kidney disease, evaluation, donor/recipient work-up, matching, living/deceased pathways, surgery, immunosuppression, rejection/infection, long-term follow-up, travel. Canopus must never broker/source organs or donors.

## Liver transplant
Separate legal/compliance review mandatory. Cover liver failure/selected cancer context, donor pathways, work-up, ICU, immunosuppression, infection, long local recovery, follow-up, cost uncertainty.

## Spine surgery
Split into lumbar stenosis, disc disease, cervical myelopathy, fusion, disc replacement, deformity and revision. Red flags such as new bowel/bladder dysfunction, rapidly progressive weakness, serious trauma or fever with severe spinal pain need urgent local assessment.

## IVF/ICSI
Review current Indian assisted-reproduction law/eligibility before publication. Cover evaluation, stimulation, monitoring, retrieval, fertilization, embryo culture, transfer, pregnancy testing, cryopreservation, cycles, medication cost, lab quality, travel timing and consent. Avoid success-rate claims unless denominator, age band, source and methodology are clear.

## Bariatric surgery
Sleeve and bypass pages: candidacy is clinical, pre-op metabolic assessment, nutrition, procedural differences, vitamins/minerals, long-term follow-up, travel and costs.

## Robotic prostate surgery
Distinguish cancer from BPH. Include pathology/staging, radical prostatectomy, robotic platform as a surgeon tool, catheter, continence, erectile function, pathology, PSA follow-up and cost.

## Cochlear implant
Hearing evaluation, hearing aids vs implant, candidacy, imaging, device, surgery, activation, mapping, auditory rehab, paediatric language development, long-term device support.
