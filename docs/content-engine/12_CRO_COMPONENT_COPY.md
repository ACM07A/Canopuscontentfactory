# 10. Trust strip

Use only verified claims.

Examples:
- `Hospital information source-verified`
- `Medical content reviewed by clinicians` only after real reviewer
- `Current estimate date shown`
- `Your consent controls hospital sharing`
- `Doctors make clinical decisions`

Avoid meaningless trust:
- “100% secure”
- “best hospitals guaranteed”
- “highest success rate”
- fake patient counts.

# 16. CRO guardrails

Never use:
- fake urgency
- fake “3 people viewing”
- misleading limited availability
- fake doctor response times
- preselected consent
- frightening symptom copy to force conversion
- “book now” immediately after emergency warning
- guaranteed savings/outcomes

Medical lead generation should convert through clarity, proof and operational help, not pressure.
