# Canopus Care creative production system

This is the operating standard for organic and paid social creative. It is a production system, not a prompt list.

## Creative promise

Every asset earns attention by reducing uncertainty for an international patient or caregiver. We do not use fear, miracle language, fake doctors, fake patients, invented credentials, or headline prices without a cited source.

## Style sheet

- Voice: calm, direct, specific, warm without reassurance theatre.
- Point of view: facilitator/coordinator; clinical decisions stay with the treating team.
- Visual world: documentary planning moments, clean editorial graphics, restrained clinical blue and care green, warm daylight, real-world texture.
- Presenter rule: use a clearly fictional AI educator or a named, verified human expert. Never imply that an invented character is a doctor.
- Typography: one strong sentence per frame, high contrast, safe margins for 9:16 crops, no dense paragraphs.
- Motion: open with a visual change in the first second; use purposeful cuts every 1.5–3 seconds; reserve the last 2 seconds for the next step and disclosure.
- Audio: spoken narration is required for a publishable reel. Captions are required and must be reviewed against the narration.
- CTA: one low-pressure action: “Request a documented coordination pathway.”

## Script standard

1. Hook: a specific question or mistake, not a generic promise.
2. Boundary: tell the viewer what the video can and cannot answer.
3. Teaching beats: three useful checks, each concrete enough to save.
4. Proof of process: show records, written responses, consent, or comparison—not a medical outcome.
5. CTA: one next step.
6. Disclosure: facilitator, general education, clinical decisions remain with qualified hospital teams.

Target lengths: 25–35 seconds for organic explainers, 15–20 seconds for paid tests, and 5–7 cards for carousels. Each hook gets at least three variants before a winner is declared.

## Production gates

- Research packet attached to the brief.
- Claim and safety audit passed.
- Character/style reference attached when a presenter is used.
- Provider model and credit cost visible before generation.
- Voiceover, captions, cover frame, aspect-ratio exports, alt text, caption copy, UTM and review record present.
- Named human reviewer approves the package before any platform publish or spend.

## Experiment loop

Ship three hook variants × two visual treatments × one CTA. Hold the body constant for the first test. Record thumb-stop rate, 3-second view rate, average watch time, completion, saves, shares, profile visits, qualified enquiries and negative feedback. Do not call a creative a winner until it has enough impressions for a pre-declared decision rule.

## Provider policy

The direct API is the repeatable production path. The Magnific web worker is the browser fallback for entitled web-only models and requires an operator-approved job. MCP actions consume credits even for models labelled unlimited by a web plan, so the factory never silently invokes MCP or clicks Generate unattended.
